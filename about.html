About the event

A gathering shaped by sacred place and living practice.

Mahamagham at Thirunavaya is presented as a spiritual and cultural gathering on the banks of the Bharathapuzha — the Nila.

The festival brings together worship, yajna, sacred bathing, learning, service, cultural expression and community participation. The 2027 website serves as the official information portal for programmes, visitor planning, live updates, media and support.

A sacred river. A living tradition. A gathering of faith, culture and service.
History

Thirunavaya, the Nila and a remembered landscape.

Thirunavaya has long held religious and cultural significance in Kerala. This portal presents the festival in connection with the sacred river, pilgrimage, ritual, memory and the continuing traditions of the region.

Historical claims and dates should be published only after validation by the organising team and approved source material.

Significance

Devotion, culture, wisdom, community and seva.

These five ideas form the public-facing experience of Mahamagham. They help visitors understand not just what happens, but why people gather and participate.
