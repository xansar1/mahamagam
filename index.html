What is Mahamagham?

A sacred gathering shaped by river, ritual and remembrance.

At Thirunavaya, the banks of the Bharathapuzha — lovingly known as the Nila — become a place of pilgrimage, yajna, prayer, sacred bathing, cultural expression and seva during the month of Magha.

Festival tradition connects this land with ancient yajnas and Kerala’s long spiritual history. Today, Mahamagham brings devotees, acharyas, volunteers and communities together in a shared experience of devotion and service.
