Thirunavaya Mahamagha Mahotsavam

The river becomes a place of gathering.

Yajna, prayer, sacred bathing, processions, Nila Aarathi, cultural traditions and seva come together during the sacred month of Magha.

What happens here

Rituals & sacred moments

01

Nila Aarathi

An evening offering of light, mantra and gratitude on the banks of the Nila.

02

Yajna & sacred chanting

Vedic rituals, chanting and collective prayer conducted under the guidance of acharyas.

03

Chariot procession

A ceremonial procession that connects communities, sacred symbols and the festival ground.

04

Sacred bathing

Holy bathing in the river on important observance days, approached with preparation and reverence.

05

Annadanam & seva

Food service, volunteer support and acts of shared service form an important part of the gathering.
