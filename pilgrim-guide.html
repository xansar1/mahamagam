Your journey to Thirunavaya

Arrive prepared. Experience with ease.

A simple visitor guide for travel, facilities, sacred days and essential festival preparation.

Plan your journey

Make your way to the sacred river.

By rail

Thirunavaya has rail connectivity. For major-route planning, visitors may also check nearby larger railway stations and official railway schedules before travel.

By road

Approach Thirunavaya through the Malappuram district road network. During festival days, follow organiser and local traffic guidance for parking and diversions.

By air

For air travel, check the most convenient Kerala airport for your route and arrange onward road or rail travel to Thirunavaya.
